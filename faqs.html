A few helpful answers

Frequently asked questions.

Here are answers to some common questions about finding a tutor and starting lessons.

How do I find the right tutor?

Start by considering your subject, level, goals, availability, and preferred learning style. Browse tutor profiles or contact us for help thinking through a match.

Which subjects and levels can I find?

Featured areas include mathematics, sciences, English, and computer science, with support from primary learning through university and adult study. Availability depends on the tutor.

Are lessons online or in person?

Online lessons can be arranged with tutors who offer them. In-person availability depends on the tutor and location, so please confirm details before booking.

How much do lessons cost?

Rates can vary by tutor, subject, and level. Please discuss pricing and lesson arrangements with your tutor before you commit.

What is the first lesson guarantee?

Our landing page describes a first-lesson guarantee, but the terms need to be confirmed by the business before bookings are accepted. Please contact us for current details.

How can I become a tutor?

Visit our For Tutors page to register your interest and share your teaching experience.
