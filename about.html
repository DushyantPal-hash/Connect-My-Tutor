Our purpose

Learning is personal. Finding support should be, too.

Every learner has their own goals, strengths, and pace. Connect My Tutor was created to make it easier to find one-to-one support that respects those differences and makes progress feel possible.

We focus on a careful match, clear communication, and practical support from the first conversation onward. Whether a student is building confidence, preparing for an exam, or exploring a new subject, the relationship with their tutor matters.

What matters to us
